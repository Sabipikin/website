Frequently Asked Questions

❓ Is this ghostwriting? Will I get in trouble?

No. SabiPath is teaching & coaching only. We review your drafts, provide feedback, and coach you on how to improve — you write and submit your own work. This keeps you safe from academic misconduct and fully complies with UK & Irish university policies.

❓ What if I don't get a good grade?

We aim for minimum B grades (or equivalent). If you receive below a B and it's due to our guidance issues, you're eligible for a 10% refund. We stand behind our coaching.

❓ Do I need to book all sessions upfront?

No. You submit assignments when they're due, request feedback 2+ weeks in advance, and book coaching sessions as needed. We work around your schedule.

❓ What if I change my topic midway?

Minor tweaks are fine. Complete topic changes are treated as new projects with adjusted fees (discussed on a case-by-case basis). Communicate early to minimize costs.

❓ Can I do extra feedback rounds if I need them?

Yes. Premium tier includes unlimited rounds. Basic/Regular tiers can purchase additional rounds at fair rates, or rush fees apply for short notice.

❓ How do payment plans work?

All tiers offer: (1) Pay full upfront, or (2) 50% now + 50% within 6 months. Plans must complete by your 6-month mark. Discuss custom arrangements if needed.
