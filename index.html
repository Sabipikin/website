Frequently Asked Questions

❓ Is this ghostwriting? Will I get in trouble? +
No. SabiPath is teaching & coaching only. We review your drafts, provide feedback, and coach you on how to improve — you write and submit your own work. This keeps you safe from academic misconduct and fully complies with UK & Irish university policies.
❓ What if I don't get a good grade? +
We aim for minimum B grades (or equivalent). If you receive below a B and it's due to our guidance issues, you're eligible for a 10% refund. We stand behind our coaching.
❓ Annual or single — which should I choose? +
Annual if you have 3+ assignments/chapters to work on — it's better value. Single Assessment if you just need help with one essay or want to try us out first. Many students start with single, then upgrade to annual.
❓ How much does a single essay review cost? +
£150–£250 depending on length and deadline. Rush fees (£50) apply for 2-3 day turnaround. Chapter reviews are £300–£450.
❓ Can I upgrade from single assessment to annual? +
Yes! If you start with a single assessment and decide you want annual coverage, we'll credit your single assessment fee toward the annual tier.
❓ How long does feedback take? +
Standard: 5-7 business days | Rush: 2-3 business days (+£50). We provide detailed written feedback + optional coaching session.
